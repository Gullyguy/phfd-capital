CAPITAL DISCLOSURES

Readiness is a path, not a promise.

Current status: PHFD Capital is operating as a sandbox-first capital-readiness and partner-routing MVP. It does not currently make, broker, approve, deny, service, or promise loans through this application.

No credit decision

Scores, bands, estimated planning ranges, HFFI profiles, partner matches, and action plans are educational and non-binding. They are not approvals, denials, offers, commitments, interest-rate quotes, or guarantees of funding.

Business-purpose boundary

The MVP is designed for business-purpose capital. It does not offer personal, household, or consumer-purpose loans.

Human review

A PHFD reviewer controls any referral. A contracted lender or capital provider controls the regulated functions assigned to it under the final agreement, which may include underwriting, lender identity, disclosures, offers, adverse-action notices, funding, servicing, collections, and complaints.

Pricing and repayment

No pricing or repayment obligation is created by this MVP. Before a live applicant accepts financing, the responsible provider must present all required product-specific terms and disclosures in a clear, reviewable form.

HFFI profile

The HFFI screen is a preliminary program-design tool. Final project, location, SNAP, use-of-funds, and funding eligibility must be confirmed by program staff, counsel, and the applicable financing partnership.

Production launch gate

Live financing remains disabled until PHFD signs provider agreements, completes legal and compliance review, approves marketing and disclosures, implements production security and privacy controls, and receives explicit applicant consent.
