# PHFD Capital Partner Outreach Packet

## Partnership proposition

Park Hill Financial District is launching **PHFD Capital**, a community-centered business capital platform serving underserved entrepreneurs, with an initial specialization in grocery ownership, healthy-food retail, food cooperatives, distribution, and post-harvest supply-chain businesses.

PHFD seeks a managed white-label or embedded-capital partner for a controlled Colorado pilot. PHFD will own community acquisition, education, capital readiness, relationship management, and initial intake. The selected provider should be capable of owning the regulated functions assigned in the agreement, including underwriting, disclosures, funding, servicing, compliance, and collections.

### Proposed pilot

- Geography: Colorado, beginning in Denver metro
- Initial cohort: 25 to 100 business customers
- Products: business-purpose working capital, term/revenue-based financing, lines of credit, equipment or inventory capital where supported
- Initial integration: provider-hosted/co-branded or white-label widget
- Later integration: API, webhooks, offer/status reporting
- Target segment: small businesses with an intentional food-capital track
- PHFD position: community platform and capital-readiness partner, not consumer lender

# Provider diligence scorecard

Score each category 1 to 5.

| Category | Questions |
|---|---|
| Partner fit | Will they accept a community organization/platform with a developing customer base? |
| Speed | Can they launch provider-hosted or co-branded before API integration? |
| Capital | Do they supply the capital? Is a reserve required? |
| Product | Which business-purpose products and amounts are available in Colorado? |
| Brand | Referral, co-brand, embedded component, or full white-label? |
| Compliance | Who owns licensing, marketing approval, disclosures, decisions, adverse action, complaints, servicing, and collections? |
| Data | What data, history, customer count, and transaction volume are required? |
| Economics | Setup fee, minimum fee, revenue share, transaction fee, exclusivity, reserve, clawbacks? |
| Technology | Widget, API, webhooks, SSO, sandbox, reporting, documentation? |
| Security | SOC report, penetration tests, encryption, incident response, subprocessor list, retention? |
| Fair lending | Model governance, reason codes, monitoring, manual review, protected-class controls? |
| Exit | Data portability, applicant communication, transition rights, termination support? |

# Thirty-minute partner call agenda

1. PHFD mission and customer segment, 3 minutes
2. Pilot structure and expected pipeline, 4 minutes
3. Provider fit and minimum thresholds, 5 minutes
4. Product/state availability and borrower eligibility, 5 minutes
5. Allocation of regulated responsibilities, 5 minutes
6. Economics and implementation, 5 minutes
7. Security, reporting, and next-step diligence, 3 minutes

# Non-negotiable contract points

- No claim that PHFD is the lender unless legally accurate
- Clear lender identity and disclosure placement
- Specific ownership of ECOA/Regulation B notices
- Marketing and communications approval process
- Applicant consent and data-use restrictions
- Security incident notification
- Data export and deletion rights
- Complaint escalation and response timelines
- No undisclosed resale of applicant leads
- State/product availability controls
- Audit and performance reporting
- Fair-lending/model-governance representations
- Termination and borrower continuity
