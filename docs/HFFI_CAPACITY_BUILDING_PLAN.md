# PHFD Food Capital
## $1 Million HFFI Capacity-Building Operating Plan

## Program identity

**Working program name:** Colorado Community Food Capital Collaborative  
**Platform:** PHFD Food Capital  
**Lead applicant candidate:** Park Hill Financial District, subject to federal grant-management readiness review  
**Public entity:** To be locked. Radiant may serve as a data/public-funding partner only if its legal status satisfies the RFA public-entity definition; do not assume that qualification.  
**Financing partner:** Managed embedded-capital provider and/or qualified lender to be selected  
**Community and food-system partners:** Metro Caring, Montbello Walks, Mo' Betta Green, Rebel in the Garden, Park Hill/Holly-area retail partners, Hope Center, and other verified participants  
**Initial geography:** Northeast Denver, Montbello, and verified eligible underserved areas in Denver metro

## Program objective

Create a multi-project Food Financing Program that prepares and connects eligible grocery stores, food cooperatives, SNAP retail outlets, mobile markets, distributors, food hubs, aggregators, processors, manufacturers, cold-storage operators, and related post-harvest enterprises to financial and technical assistance.

The application must not be framed as funding one co-op grocery store. The co-op belongs in the current project pipeline as one potential eligible project.

## Capacity building versus credit enhancement

### Capacity Building lane

- Program personnel and benefits
- Partnership administration
- Data and community food-access analysis
- Financial-product design
- Lender/provider recruitment
- Technical assistance to eligible projects
- Business pipeline development
- Outreach, meetings, site visits, and marketing
- Program software and application materials
- Staff training
- Legal, tax, and accounting work to launch the financing program
- Evaluation and reporting infrastructure

### Credit Enhancement lane, separate application/budget decision

- Loan loss reserve
- Loan guarantee
- Interest-rate buy-down
- Collateral support
- Flexible credit policy support
- Closing-cost subsidy
- Grants made to eligible projects under the authorized structure

A qualified lender must be formally included if Credit Enhancement is requested. PHFD should not add this lane until the lender, underwriting, servicing, leverage, and federal compliance mechanics are fully committed.

## Partnership roles

### PHFD

- Program convener
- Capital-readiness platform owner
- Community pipeline development
- Financial education and technical assistance
- Partnership management
- Impact and HFFI reporting coordination

### Public entity

- Public-private partnership eligibility
- Data, research, evaluation, public funding alignment, or program participation
- Signed commitment and MOU
- No unnecessary control of PHFD's community model

### Qualified lender / embedded capital provider

- Financial-product design
- Underwriting and funding
- Required disclosures and decision notices
- Servicing and portfolio performance
- Data sharing and reporting
- Credit Enhancement administration if requested

### Food-system partners

- Recruit eligible projects
- Validate local market needs
- Provide subject-matter expertise
- Participate in program design
- Deliver eligible technical-assistance activities when structured and budgeted correctly

### Data partner

- Underserved-area analysis
- Food-access gaps
- Capital-access gaps
- Business and project mapping
- Program metrics and dashboards

## First pipeline categories

1. Community-owned grocery cooperative
2. Existing neighborhood grocer acquisition or expansion
3. SNAP-authorized mobile healthy-food market
4. Grocery refrigeration and energy-resilience upgrade
5. Local food aggregator serving SNAP retailers
6. Wholesale distributor expanding into underserved retail routes
7. Cold-storage or last-mile delivery enterprise
8. Food hub or light processor connected to qualifying retailers
9. BIPOC-owned healthy-food retailer
10. Women-owned food-retail or post-harvest supply-chain enterprise
