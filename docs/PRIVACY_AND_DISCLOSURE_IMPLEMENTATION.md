# Privacy and Disclosure Implementation Gate

The MVP includes public demonstration pages at `/privacy` and `/disclosures`. They are architecture placeholders, not final legal documents.

Before live launch:

- Identify the legal controller, processor, lender, servicer, and referral/broker roles.
- Replace the demonstration privacy notice with a counsel-approved notice tied to actual data flows.
- Version and timestamp every applicant consent.
- Disclose the identity of every provider that receives applicant data.
- Document the fields shared, purpose, retention, deletion, and revocation process.
- Implement verified access, correction, deletion, export, opt-out, complaint, and incident-response workflows.
- Approve product-specific business-financing disclosures and marketing claims.
- Assign ownership for ECOA/Regulation B notices, where applicable.
- Store accepted offer terms and disclosures in an immutable audit record.
- Complete privacy, security, fair-lending, vendor, and penetration-test reviews.
